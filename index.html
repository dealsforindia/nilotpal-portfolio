02Studio

Behind the work.

A designer, a desk in South Kolkata, and a practice built on print. The short version — and the receipts.

I'm Nilotpal Saha — a senior graphic designer and video editor based in Subhasgram, Kolkata.

Since 2019 I've worked inside studios and agencies, moving from ad creatives and brand collateral to leading creative at Image Clinic. My work sits where print craft meets everyday communication: posters, identities, social campaigns and video.

I care about type that reads, colour that behaves, and work that looks like it was made by a person — because it was.
